## Bank Obligations Upon Receipt of an EFT Error Notice

Under 12 CFR 1005.11, a financial institution must comply with the error resolution procedures with respect to any oral or written notice of error from the consumer [C:exam_manual-p700-c0]. Once such notice is received, the bank must follow these steps and timelines:

### 1. Investigate Promptly — 10 Business Day Standard
The bank "shall investigate promptly and... shall determine whether an error occurred within 10 business days of receiving a notice of error" [C:12CFR1005.11-c1].

### 2. Report Results — 3 Business Days After Completing Investigation
The bank must "report the results of its investigation within three business days after completing its investigation" [C:exam_manual-p701-c0] [C:12CFR1005.11-c1].

### 3. Correct the Error — 1 Business Day After Determination
The bank must "correct the error within one business day after determining that an error has occurred" [C:exam_manual-p701-c0] [C:12CFR1005.11-c1].

### 4. Extended 45-Day Investigation (If Needed)
If the bank cannot complete the investigation within 10 business days, it "may take up to 45 calendar days... to complete its investigation," but only if it:
- "Provisionally credits the funds (including interest, where applicable) to the consumer's account within the 10 business-day period"
- "Advises the consumer within two business days of the provisional crediting"
- "Gives the consumer full use of the funds during the investigation" [C:exam_manual-p701-c0]

**Exception:** The bank need not provisionally credit the account to take the extended 45-day period if the consumer fails to provide required written confirmation of an oral notice of error, or if the account is subject to Regulation T margin requirements [C:exam_manual-p701-c0].

**Unauthorized EFT caveat:** Where an error involves an unauthorized EFT, additional requirements apply [C:exam_manual-p701-c0].

### 5. Extended Timeframes for New Accounts/Certain Transactions
- If the error occurred within 30 days after the first deposit to the account, the 10- and 45-day periods extend to **20 and 90 days**, respectively.
- If the error involves a transaction not initiated in a state, or a point-of-sale debit card transaction, the 45-day period extends to **90 days** [C:exam_manual-p702-c0].

### 6. Required Content of Results Notice
When reporting investigation results, if the bank determines no error (or a different error) occurred, the available evidence indicates the bank must provide the determination **and** an "explanation of the determination"—meaning it must "go beyond just providing the findings to actually explain or give the reasons for or cause of those findings" [C:sh_issue22-p10-c0]. Separately, upon the consumer's request, the bank must mail or deliver copies of documents it relied on in making its determination [C:exam_manual-p702-c0].

### 7. If Debiting Provisional Credit
If the bank later debits a provisionally credited amount (after determining no error occurred), it must notify the consumer of the date and amount of the debit, and must honor (without charge) checks, drafts, or preauthorized debits for **five business days** after transmittal of that notice (limited to items it would have paid absent the debit) [C:exam_manual-p702-c0].

### 8. Document Production
Upon consumer request, the bank must "promptly mail or deliver to the consumer copies of documents upon which it relied in making its determination" [C:exam_manual-p702-c0].
