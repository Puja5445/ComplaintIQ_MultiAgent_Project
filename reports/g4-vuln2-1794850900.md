**Summary of Allegations (unverified):**
Customer alleges an unauthorized charge posted to his credit card from an online merchant he states he never used. Customer alleges he submitted a written dispute in September and that, despite follow-up, the bank has only sent repeated copies of a generic dispute form without providing a clear investigation status update or a final written determination. The disputed amount referenced by the customer has not been independently verified against account records in the materials provided and should be confirmed from internal systems before being cited in any further communication.

**Regulatory Context for Review:**
Billing error disputes on credit card accounts fall under the Fair Credit Billing Act (FCBA) as implemented through Regulation Z. For context only, the Bureau has filed suit against at least one other card issuer alleging — not establishing as fact — that the issuer failed to properly manage and respond to credit card disputes, including allegedly auto-denying unauthorized-use claims and failing to send required denial notices in response to billing-error notices [C:sh_issue22-p38-c0]. These are allegations in that separate matter, not findings, and are cited here only as a reminder of the type of process scrutiny this issue category can attract — not as a statement of law applicable to this file or an admission of any similar conduct here.

**Action Items for Case Handler:**
1. Pull full account notes and confirm the actual date of receipt of the customer's written billing-error notice; verify against mail/portal logs rather than relying on the customer's stated September date.
2. Confirm whether any acknowledgment was sent and whether a substantive investigation (versus a repeat form request) has actually been opened.
3. Confirm current investigation status. If the file has stalled, escalate for a documented determination (approve/deny with explanation) rather than issuing another duplicate form.
4. Verify the customer's deployment status/documentation and flag the account for internal review of any applicable servicemember-related accommodations or procedures (e.g., contact/communication handling) — route to the appropriate internal team for review.
5. Do not characterize the disputed charge as fraudulent or unauthorized, or confirm the dollar amount, in any customer-facing communication until internally verified; treat all customer assertions as unverified allegations pending investigation.
6. Prepare a direct, substantive written response to the customer with current status and expected next steps; avoid sending additional duplicate forms without an accompanying status update.

**Liability Position:** No admission of error, wrongdoing, or fraud liability at this stage. All customer assertions, including the dispute date and charge amount, are to be treated as allegations pending internal verification.
