## Account-Opening Disclosures Under Truth in Savings (Regulation DD)

**Timing of Delivery**

A depository institution must provide account disclosures to a consumer before an account is opened or a service is provided, whichever is earlier [C:12CFR1030.4-c0]. An institution is deemed to have provided a service when a fee required to be disclosed is assessed [C:12CFR1030.4-c0].

- If the consumer is **not present** at the institution when the account is opened or service provided, and has not already received disclosures, the institution must mail or deliver disclosures **no later than 10 business days** after the account is opened or service provided, whichever is earlier [C:12CFR1030.4-c0].
- If a consumer who is not present uses **electronic means** (e.g., a website) to open an account or request a service, disclosures must be provided **before** the account is opened or the service is provided [C:12CFR1030.4-c0] [C:exam_manual-p754-c0].

**Disclosures Upon Request**

A depository institution must also provide account disclosures to a consumer upon request [C:12CFR1030.4-c0]:
- If the consumer is present, disclosures must be provided at that time [C:exam_manual-p754-c0].
- A response to an **oral inquiry** (by phone or in person) about rates, yields, or fees does **not** trigger the duty to provide written account disclosures; however, when a consumer asks for **written information** about an account, the institution must provide disclosures, unless the account is no longer offered to the public [C:exam_manual-p754-c0].

**General Form Requirements**

Separately, disclosures under related consumer protection rules (e.g., Regulation E) must be clear and readily understandable, in writing, and in a form the consumer may keep, and may be combined with disclosures required by other laws such as the Truth in Savings Act [C:12CFR1005.4-c0].
