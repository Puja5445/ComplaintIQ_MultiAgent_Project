## EVIDENCE: Why This Matters
Even in the absence of data, examiners and compliance teams are expected to maintain robust complaint-handling infrastructure for deposit accounts:

- Under EFTA/Regulation E, when a financial institution debits a provisionally credited amount, it must notify the consumer of the date and amount of the debit, and must honor (without charge) checks, drafts, and preauthorized debits payable to third parties for five business days after the notice is transmitted. Consumers may also request copies of documents the institution relied on in making its determination [C:exam_manual-p702-c0].
- If an error notice involves a transaction occurring within 30 days of the first deposit to the account, the standard 10/45-day investigation windows are extended to 20/90 days; if the transaction was not initiated in a state or arose from a point-of-sale debit card transaction, the 45-day period is extended to 90 days [C:exam_manual-p702-c0].
- Separately, Regulation E overdraft opt-in rules require institutions to provide consumers who do not affirmatively consent to ATM/one-time debit card overdraft service the **same account terms, conditions, and features** as consenting consumers (excluding that overdraft coverage). For accounts opened prior to July 1, 2010, institutions may not assess fees for paying ATM/one-time debit transactions under the overdraft service on or after August 15, 2010, unless affirmative consent was obtained [C:12CFR1005.17-c1].
- An effective compliance management system should ensure the entity is **responsive and responsible** in handling consumer complaints and inquiries, and that intelligence gathered from consumer contacts is organized, retained, and used as part of the institution's compliance management system [C:exam_manual-p43-c0].
- CFPB risk assessment templates flag recurring complaint themes — including unauthorized or duplicative fees, previously undisclosed charges, misleading or false statements, and customer service — as inputs to a Low/Moderate/High consumer complaint risk rating [C:exam_manual-p872-c0].

*Note: Mortgage-servicing-specific examination procedures (e.g., direct consumer interviews to assess complaint resolution) were reviewed but are not included here, as they pertain to mortgage servicing rather than checking accounts [C:exam_manual-p140-c0].*
