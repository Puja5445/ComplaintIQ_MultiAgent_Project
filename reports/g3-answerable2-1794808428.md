## Furnisher's Duties After Receiving a Direct Dispute

**1. General Duty to Investigate**

Except as otherwise provided, a furnisher must conduct a reasonable investigation of a direct dispute if it relates to:
- The consumer's liability for a credit account or other debt with the furnisher, including disputes relating to identity theft or fraud, individual or joint liability, or authorized user status [C:12CFR1022.43-c0];
- The terms of a credit account or other debt, such as the type of account, principal balance, scheduled payment amount, or credit limit [C:12CFR1022.43-c0];
- The consumer's performance or other conduct concerning an account, such as current payment status, high balance, date or amount of a payment made, or date an account was opened or closed [C:12CFR1022.43-c0];
- Any other information in a consumer report regarding an account or relationship with the furnisher that bears on the consumer's creditworthiness, credit standing, cr[...] [C:12CFR1022.43-c0] (the evidence text is cut off at this point, but the regulation specifies this as a fourth category of covered disputes).

**2. Where the Dispute Must Be Submitted**

The furnisher is only required to investigate if the dispute notice is submitted to:
1. The address provided by the furnisher and listed on a consumer report relating to the consumer;
2. An address clearly and conspicuously specified by the furnisher in writing or electronically (if the consumer agreed to electronic delivery); or
3. Any business address of the furnisher, if no specific address has been designated [C:12CFR1022.43-c1].

**3. Required Contents of the Dispute Notice**

A valid dispute notice must include:
1. Sufficient information to identify the account or relationship in dispute (e.g., account number, consumer's name, address, phone number);
2. The specific information being disputed and an explanation of the basis for the dispute [C:12CFR1022.43-c1].

**4. If the Investigation Finds Inaccurate Information**

If the investigation finds that the information reported was inaccurate, the furnisher must promptly notify each consumer reporting agency to which it provided the inaccurate information of the investigation findings, and provide any correction necessary to make the information accurate [C:12CFR1022.43-c2] [C:exam_manual-p638-c0].

**5. Exception – Frivolous or Irrelevant Disputes**

A furnisher is not required to investigate if it reasonably determines the dispute is frivolous or irrelevant. A dispute qualifies as frivolous or irrelevant if:
- The consumer did not provide sufficient information to investigate the disputed information as required by the notice-content provisions [C:12CFR1022.43-c2];
- The dispute is substantially the same as one previously submitted (directly or through a CRA) for which the furnisher already satisfied applicable requirements — unless the new dispute includes required information not previously provided [C:12CFR1022.43-c2]; or
- The furnisher is not required to investigate the direct dispute because one or more of the exceptions listed in the section applies [C:12CFR1022.43-c2].

**6. Other Exceptions**

The direct dispute requirements do not apply if the furnisher reasonably believes the dispute was submitted by, prepared on behalf of, or submitted on a form supplied by a credit repair organization [C:exam_manual-p637-c0].
