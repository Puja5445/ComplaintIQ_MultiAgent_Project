# ComplaintIQ report g3-answerable8-1794808662

A cardholder's liability for unauthorized use of a credit card "shall not exceed the lesser of $50 or the amount of money, property, labor, or services obtained by the unauthorized use before notification to the card issuer" [C:12CFR1026.12-c0].

A cardholder may be held liable for unauthorized use at all only if certain conditions are met, including that the credit card is an accepted credit card and the card issuer has provided adequate notice to the cardholder [C:12CFR1026.12-c0].

Additionally, if state law or an agreement between the cardholder and card issuer imposes a lesser liability than the $50 cap, that lesser liability governs instead [C:12CFR1026.12-c1].
