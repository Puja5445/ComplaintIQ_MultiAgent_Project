# Data card - ComplaintIQ

All data is public. The bank (Harbourline Bank) is fictional.

## Sources
| ID | Dataset | Publisher / access | Format | Used for | Fetched by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Consumer Complaint Database: Credit card and Checking or savings account, Jan-Jun 2026, narrative filter on | CFPB public search API, no key | 2 CSV files, 15 columns | SQL analytics, triage labels and ground truth, similar-case index | `data.py` |
| D2 | eCFR Title 12, Parts 1005 (Reg E), 1026 (Reg Z), 1022 (Reg V), 1006 (Reg F), 1030 (Reg DD) | Office of the Federal Register, versioner API | 5 XML files | Regulatory retrieval (by section) | `data.py` |
| D3 | CFPB Supervisory Highlights issues 17, 18, 22, 23 and the Supervision and Examination Manual v2 | CFPB, files.consumerfinance.gov | 5 PDF files | Supervisory findings (by page) | `data.py` |
| D4 | 360 synthetic complaint narratives (300 Jan-May, 60 June) | Generated by us from the real D1 labels | `data/processed/narratives.jsonl` | Triage input, similar-case index, G1 test | `core.py: build_narratives()` |

Each downloaded file is recorded in `data/manifest.json` (url, bytes, sha256, UTC time). The download is re-runnable and skips files that exist.

## Filters and cleaning
- Products: Credit card; Checking or savings account. Window: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30. Narrative-present filter requested from the API.
- DuckDB: `complaints_raw` holds both CSVs as text; `v_complaints` is the typed view (12 columns: complaint_id, date_received, product, subproduct, issue, subissue, company, state, tags, submitted_via, company_response, timely). Duplicate complaint ids are removed with `ROW_NUMBER()`.
- Size at first download: about 93,711 rows (48,863 credit card, 44,848 checking/savings). A later download gave 93,710; CFPB updates its data.
- Chunks: 1,759 (paragraph boundaries, up to 3,000 characters, 400 overlap, median 2,474). Ids look like `12CFR1005.11-c0` and `sh_issue22-p14-c1`.

## Leakage-free split
- Index pool: complaints received Jan-May 2026 (78,573 rows). Feeds the similar-case index and few-shot examples.
- Hold-out: June 2026 (15,138 rows). Used only for evaluation and never put into any index.

## Synthetic narratives (D4)
- Why: the CFPB API and CSV no longer return the *Consumer complaint narrative* column; only the 15 label columns come back.
- How: the real labels (product, subproduct, issue, subissue, company, state, tags, date) go to Amazon Nova Lite, which writes one 3-5 sentence first-person complaint. The prompt forbids the label words. Older American and Servicemember tags become natural hints. About 1 story in 10 carries a fake phone number and card ending to exercise the PII guard.
- Why a different model: triage uses Claude. A Claude-written story would be graded by Claude reading its own style.
- Fixes: some replies contained several complaints or were blocked by content filters; they were removed and regenerated. Final file has 360 clean stories.
- Raw CSV is never changed. Stories are joined to labels by complaint id in the view `v_narratives` (`join_narratives.py`).

## Licence
As stated in the project brief: CFPB complaint data under the API's own CC0 terms; eCFR and CFPB publications are U.S. government works. The synthetic narratives are fictional and contain no real personal data.

## Personal data
CFPB removes personal information from published data. The synthetic stories use fake details only. The guard still redacts SSN, card/account numbers, email and phone patterns at run time.

## Known biases and limits
- Complaints are self-selected by consumers and skew toward large companies and a few issue types.
- Synthetic narratives are cleaner and more consistent than real ones, and were written from the labels, so triage scores on them are optimistic.
- The regulation corpus covers five regulations plus four Supervisory Highlights issues and the exam manual. It does not cover mortgages, capital rules or a bank's own policies, so such questions should abstain. The exam manual makes up most chunks (about 61% in our first chunking).
- Only two products and six months are covered.
